Particle Explorer

An interactive 3D particle system built with Three.js and WebGL. Thousands of particles are distributed across a 3D surface and respond to your cursor in real time.

How it works

  • Particles are sampled uniformly across the triangles of a 3D geometry using area-weighted random sampling
  • Each particle has a home position on the surface; a spring force constantly pulls it back
  • When the cursor comes within the repel radius, an outward force pushes particles away — the result is the fluid scatter effect
  • Velocity is dampened each frame (×0.82) so particles settle smoothly rather than oscillating

Shapes

  • Sphere — particles distributed across a subdivided sphere (64×64 segments)
  • Torus — a donut shape; the inner ring has lower triangle density so sampling is area-weighted to stay even
  • Box — six subdivided faces; particles concentrate along edges where triangles meet

Desktop controls

  • Particle Count — more particles = higher fidelity surface, heavier CPU cost
  • Repel Radius / Strength — how far and how forcefully the cursor disturbs the field
  • Particle Size — rendered as screen-space billboard sprites with size attenuation
  • Scroll to zoom · click and drag to rotate the shape freely

Mobile gestures

  • Swipe left / right — cycles through the three shapes
  • Touch and drag — repels particles from your finger
  • Pinch — zooms the camera in and out
