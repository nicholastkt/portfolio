Generative Interactive Three.js

Interactive Particles

A real-time 3D particle system built with Three.js where thousands of particles are distributed uniformly across the surface of geometric shapes — sphere, torus, and box — using area-weighted triangle sampling. Each particle is anchored to a home position by a spring force and scattered outward when the cursor draws near, producing a fluid organic repel effect. On desktop, a live control panel lets you tune particle count, repel radius, strength, and size in real time. On mobile, swipe left or right to cycle between shapes and touch to disturb the field.

Interactive WebGL React

Ink Simulation

A real-time fluid simulation that recreates the swirling of ink dropped into water. Originally a React component from the react-bits library, it was ported to vanilla JavaScript to allow deep exploration and customisation. The simulation runs entirely on the GPU through a pipeline of GLSL shaders — curl, vorticity confinement, divergence, pressure solving, and advection — implementing an incompressible Navier-Stokes fluid solver. A live settings panel on desktop exposes every parameter, and the display shader dynamically switches between additive and subtractive rendering to maintain contrast against any background colour.
